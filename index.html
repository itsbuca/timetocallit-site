How It Settles · Gift Market

Everybody's funds come home. Winners get the gift.

In the U.S. Gift market, Backing is never a wager — it's support. Nobody can lose their money.

Winning side

No Backer's funds are ever at risk. Every Backer on the winning side gets their full Backing funds returned, and the winning CALLER earns the gift.

Funds returned · Winning CALLER earns the gift
Losing side

Every Backer gets their full Backing funds returned. Nothing is lost.

Funds returned to losing Backers

Backers never win a prize — win or lose, their funds come back. A Backer's reward is earning the right to become a CALLER. Because no funds are ever at risk, this is participation, not wagering — it reaches the four-in-five who would never open a sportsbook.

Winning side

Every Backer on the winning side gets their Backing funds returned. The winning CALLER wins the cash pot. The pot pays from the smaller side — its Backer count times the Back amount. Example: 240 Backers at $1 pays the CALLER $240.

Funds returned · Winning CALLER wins the cash
Losing side

Backers on the losing side forfeit their Backing funds to the winning CALLER. This is real-money play, in licensed jurisdictions only.

Funds forfeited by losing Backers → to the Winner

There is no gift on the cash side — the prize is cash. Backers never win the pot; only the winning CALLER does. The pot always pays from the smaller side, so both sides are matched. When one side has more Backers, the extra funds go to the Bonus Pool — which funds the annual CALL-IT Poker Tournament in Curaçao that every Founding Player qualifies for. Cash play runs only where licensed.

The house never takes a side and never sets odds — it earns the same small fee on every Backing, win or lose. It is structurally impossible for the house to profit from any Backer's loss. Cash play runs only where licensed.
